**Category:** Tokens  
**Status:** Stable

### Background Colors

| Variable | Light value | Dark value | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-bg-canvas`   | `slate-50`  | `slate-950` | Page/app background |

| `--color-bg-elevated` | `white`     | `slate-800` | Dropdowns, popovers |
